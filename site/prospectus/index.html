Prospectus

Do what thou wilt shall be the whole of the Law.

The Task of this grade (Student) is to obtain “a general intellectual knowledge of all systems of attainment” (e.g., artistic, religious, scientific, and philosophical systems used in our theurgy). That is of course a lifelong journey with no visible end! As such, the work will be ongoing but will have benchmarks to indicate readiness for additionally taking on further Grade work of the A∴A∴ in Probationer Oaths & Tasks per Liber 185.

The course of study

This initial level of research is general in scope, and as such it will be a wide survey using perspectives and projects within humanities and the sciences. As a Student, one will prepare for later grade work of the A∴A∴ through assisted independent study of:

  • readings from the classical A∴A∴ reading lists and select later texts
  • brief discussions of readings, and later, deeper literature reviews
  • contextual history & cultural studies as needed
  • philosophical roots of magical theories and practices
  • world religions & mythologies
  • tutorials in scientific methodology
  • tutorials in art theory & practice
  • synthesizing earlier lessons in preparation for personal research & development

Even if one chooses not to continue in A∴A∴ after doing some work in the AASC, our aim is to at least help one obtain some useful enrichment that may aid their going nonetheless.
